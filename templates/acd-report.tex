% ACD (Aprendizaje en Contacto con el Docente) report template: the approved
% "session record sheet" design (proposal ACD-B, aa-acd-templates S1/S3).
%   * A4 portrait, no cover page. Every page carries a header with the faculty
%     logo at the left and the record identification at the right ("FEIRNNR ·
%     CARRERA DE COMPUTACIÓN" / "REGISTRO DE SESIÓN · ACD-N") above a brick
%     red rule, and a running footer with the student(s), the subject and
%     "page / total".
%   * Body font IBM Plex Sans 10pt, flush left; IBM Plex Mono spaced capitals
%     for every label; graphite ink with brick red (#9C2F2A, from the faculty
%     crest) as the only accent and cool greys for rules.
%   * Section 1 is the session block the renderer generates from metadata (see
%     acd_session_block); the remaining fixed sections are authored in body.md.
%     Pandoc definition lists in body.md ("term" then ": definition") render as
%     the mono label column of the mockup (the `description` list below).
%   * The bibliography is numbered like any section, and the annexes render
%     after it through the renderer's after-bibliography slot.
%   * Unlike the institutional templates (onehalfspacing), the sheet keeps the
%     mockup's tight 1.08 line spacing.
